Scope of work

What the pilot covers

  1. Capability profile for your pack

    Every parameter your technicians can read or write is defined against the JBD SP24S004 with its real minimum, maximum and danger level. A value outside those bounds is refused by the server, not merely hidden in the app.

    Weeks 1–2

  2. Two gateways commissioned

    Bluetooth gateways paired to your packs and registered to MEB Energy. A gateway that is revoked stops working immediately and frees its slot.

    Week 3

  3. Twenty seats, issued by you

    You create and remove your own users, including short-lived guest accounts for a contractor solving one problem. A user who has written to a pack is suspended rather than deleted, because the audit trail must keep naming who did what.

    Week 3

  4. Technician training and handover

    Two half-day sessions on site, covering the read flow, the write confirmation, and what the app refuses to do and why.

    Week 4

  5. Assisted remote support

    With a technician present at the pack, our engineers can read and change parameters during a support session. Every change is written to an append-only ledger naming the engineer, the value before and after, and the moment it happened.

    Ongoing

Terms

How the engagement runs

Term
Twelve months from the day access is switched on, not from the date of this quotation.
Seats
Twenty concurrent users. Guest accounts count while active and release their seat when removed.
Devices
The MEB Energy owner account may be signed in on two devices at once. A third sign-in signs out the least recently used one and says which.
Changing tier
Seats and gateways can be raised mid-term at a pro-rated rate. The renewal date does not move.
Data
Telemetry and the audit ledger belong to MEB Energy and are exportable at any point, including on exit.
Payment
Raised on activation, thirty days net. Access is enabled once payment is recorded.
