The four rules

Apply these and any new screen lands in the same language.

  1. 1Two materials, two meanings. Leather = live instrumentation and stays pinned. Cream = data and controls and always scrolls.
  2. 2The stitch is the only border. Dashed, inset 9 pt. No card hairlines, no Material shadows.
  3. 3Digits outrank the needle. Every gauge ships a numeric readout with no prop able to suppress it — a misread protection threshold is a fire risk.
  4. 4Detail data stays tabular. Cells, faults and parameters are rows. Gauges never appear on a list screen.
